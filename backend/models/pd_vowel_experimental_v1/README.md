# pd_vowel_experimental_v1 — Parkinson's sustained-vowel classifier (EXPERIMENTAL)

> **Research prototype. Not a diagnostic device.** The output is a task-specific
> score used only as one input to a reviewed follow-up rule (PD-03); it never
> raises a finding on its own and must never be presented as a diagnosis.

Generated by `ml_training/train_pd_vowel.py` on 2026-10-17T07:20:27+00:00.

## Intended use
- Research / hackathon prototype in a health-advocate app: estimate whether a
  sustained /a/ phonation resembles recordings from people with Parkinson's in
  the UCI dataset, as a *supporting* signal for suggesting a clinical follow-up.
- Out of scope: diagnosis, screening of the general population, severity
  estimation, any clinical decision, non-English speakers, connected speech.

## Training data
- UCI Parkinson's dataset (Little et al., IEEE TBME 2009; BioMed Eng OnLine 2007):
  **195 recordings; documented as 31 subjects (23 with PD)**, ~6 sustained-vowel
  phonations per subject. **Subject-ID caveat:** the dataset documentation says
  31 people (23 PD), but deriving the subject from the `name` column
  (`phon_R01_S01_1` → `phon_R01_S01`) yields 32 name-derived IDs (24 PD-labelled, 8 healthy);
  three IDs (S21, S27, S35) have 7 recordings instead of 6. We group CV by the 32 IDs,
  so if one person carries two IDs, at most one person may have recordings on both
  sides of a fold. This is *less* strict than true per-person grouping, and the
  reported grouped-CV AUC may therefore be very slightly optimistic.
- Sustained vowel only; English speakers (recorded in Denver, CO, USA); studio
  conditions; small, single-site sample with no external validation.
- Features: the 22 published dysphonia measures — `MDVP:Fo(Hz)`, `MDVP:Fhi(Hz)`, `MDVP:Flo(Hz)`, `MDVP:Jitter(%)`, `MDVP:Jitter(Abs)`, `MDVP:RAP`, `MDVP:PPQ`, `Jitter:DDP`, `MDVP:Shimmer`, `MDVP:Shimmer(dB)`, `Shimmer:APQ3`, `Shimmer:APQ5`, `MDVP:APQ`, `Shimmer:DDA`, `NHR`, `HNR`, `RPDE`, `DFA`, `spread1`, `spread2`, `D2`, `PPE`.

## Model
- Candidates: StandardScaler + SelectKBest + LogisticRegression, and XGBoost (depth ≤ 3, ≤ 300 trees).
- Chosen: **logreg** (higher pooled grouped-CV AUC); final hyper-parameters `{"clf__C": 0.01, "select__k": "all"}`.
- Probabilities calibrated with sigmoid `CalibratedClassifierCV` on subject-grouped splits.

## Evaluation (subject-grouped, nested)
- Outer: GroupKFold(n_splits=5) by subject. Inner: StratifiedGroupKFold(n_splits<=4) by subject for GridSearchCV and sigmoid CalibratedClassifierCV. Feature selection, hyper-parameter
  search and calibration happen only inside outer training folds.
- 95% CI: subject-cluster bootstrap of the pooled out-of-fold AUC.

| model | pooled OOF AUC | 95% CI | repeated CV AUC (mean ± sd) | sens @ 80% spec | Brier | subject-level AUC (median prob) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| logreg | 0.736 | 0.531–0.908 | 0.793 ± 0.026 (n=10) | 0.497 | 0.164 | 0.755 |
| xgboost | 0.544 | 0.293–0.790 | 0.597 ± 0.077 (n=10) | 0.122 | 0.189 | 0.536 |

Chosen model:
- Serving threshold (80% specificity on out-of-fold recordings): **0.8387**
  → sensitivity 0.497 at specificity 0.812 (recording level);
  subject level (median probability): sensitivity 0.500, specificity 0.750.
- Confusion at calibrated 0.5 (recordings): TN=19, FP=29, FN=13, TP=134.

With only 8 healthy subjects, every specificity estimate rests on very few
people; the CIs are wide and the threshold is unstable.

## Serving caveat — app scores are experimental / display-only
> **The metrics above were measured on the UCI feature table, not on audio.**
> In the app, features are computed from phone recordings by
> `app/ml/features/vowel.py` using Praat (parselmouth) plus our own
> re-implementations of RPDE, DFA, PPE, spread1/spread2 and D2. `spread2`, `D2`
> and `NHR` are known to be on a **different scale** from the UCI/MDVP training
> features, and **no validation on real audio exists** (no recording has been run
> through both pipelines). App probabilities are therefore experimental,
> display-only values: they are never a diagnosis, never raise a finding alone
> (rule PD-03 requires an independent supporting rule), and their calibration and
> threshold should not be assumed to transfer from the table above.

## Serving-time feature differences (stated limitation)
Training features were extracted with Kay MDVP and Little's MATLAB code on studio
recordings. At serving, features are computed by `app/ml/features/vowel.py` with
Praat (parselmouth) and re-implementations on phone recordings. These are **not
validated to be interchangeable**; expect a domain shift.

| feature | approximate | our method | difference from original |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MDVP:Fo(Hz)` | no | Praat To Pitch (ac), 75-600 Hz; mean of voiced frames | MDVP pitch extraction differs from Praat's autocorrelation tracker; means agree closely on clean vowels. |
| `MDVP:Fhi(Hz)` | yes | max of Praat voiced pitch frames | Extremes are sensitive to octave errors; MDVP's tracker and windowing differ, so Fhi/Flo are only approximately comparable. |
| `MDVP:Flo(Hz)` | yes | min of Praat voiced pitch frames | See MDVP:Fhi(Hz). |
| `MDVP:Jitter(%)` | no | Praat Get jitter (local) on PointProcess (periodic, cc), as a fraction (UCI stores the fraction, e.g. 0.0078) | Praat manual states jitter (local) corresponds to MDVP Jitt; period detection algorithm differs. |
| `MDVP:Jitter(Abs)` | no | Praat Get jitter (local, absolute), seconds | Corresponds to MDVP Jita. |
| `MDVP:RAP` | no | Praat Get jitter (rap) | Corresponds to MDVP RAP. |
| `MDVP:PPQ` | no | Praat Get jitter (ppq5) | Corresponds to MDVP PPQ (5-point). |
| `Jitter:DDP` | no | Praat Get jitter (ddp) (= 3 x RAP) | Definitional equivalent. |
| `MDVP:Shimmer` | no | Praat Get shimmer (local) | Corresponds to MDVP Shim. |
| `MDVP:Shimmer(dB)` | no | Praat Get shimmer (local_dB) | Corresponds to MDVP ShdB. |
| `Shimmer:APQ3` | no | Praat Get shimmer (apq3) | Definitional equivalent. |
| `Shimmer:APQ5` | no | Praat Get shimmer (apq5) | Definitional equivalent. |
| `MDVP:APQ` | no | Praat Get shimmer (apq11) | Praat manual: apq11 corresponds to MDVP APQ. |
| `Shimmer:DDA` | no | Praat Get shimmer (dda) (= 3 x APQ3) | Definitional equivalent. |
| `NHR` | yes | 10 ** (-HNR / 10) from Praat harmonicity (cc) | MDVP NHR is an energy ratio of 1.5-4.5 kHz inharmonic to 70-4.5 kHz harmonic energy; the HNR conversion is a different (broadband) quantity with a different scale. |
| `HNR` | no | Praat To Harmonicity (cc), 0.01 s, 75 Hz, mean in dB | UCI HNR came from Little's code / MDVP-family processing; Praat HNR is the standard equivalent. |
| `RPDE` | yes | Recurrence period density entropy (Little 2007): signal resampled to 25 kHz, normalised to [-1,1], time-delay embedded (m=4, tau=35), close returns within eps=0.12 up to T_max=1000 samples, normalised Shannon entropy H/ln(T_max) on a central 0.3 s segment | Embedding parameters are our reconstruction from the paper; Little's code also pre-filtered the signal and used the whole recording. |
| `DFA` | yes | Detrended fluctuation analysis (Little 2007) of the 25 kHz signal, window sizes L=50..100 step 10, scaling exponent alpha, reported as logistic 1/(1+exp(-alpha)) | Window range and logistic transform as described in Little 2007; exact segment/overlap choices of the original code unknown. |
| `spread1` | yes | ln(std) of the whitened semitone pitch residual (AR(2) linear prediction), residual in octaves | No public algorithmic definition of spread1/spread2 exists; this is our closest documented approximation, chosen to measure nonlinear F0 variation on a comparable scale (UCI range about -7.9 to -2.4). |
| `spread2` | yes | std of the mean-removed semitone pitch contour, in octaves | No public definition; approximation on a comparable scale (UCI range about 0.006 to 0.45). |
| `D2` | yes | Grassberger-Procaccia correlation dimension: 25 kHz signal decimated x2, embedding m=10, tau=8, 1500 sampled points, Theiler window 50, slope of log C(r) vs log r between the 2nd and 30th percentiles of pairwise distances | Embedding and scaling-region choices are ours; correlation-dimension estimates are highly parameter dependent. |
| `PPE` | yes | Pitch period entropy (Little 2009): pitch to semitones re 127.09 Hz, AR(2) whitening, entropy of residual histogram (0.1-semitone bins over +/-3 semitones) normalised by ln(n_bins) | Histogram binning and normalisation are our choices; Little used a different probability-density estimate. |

## Limitations
- 31 people (32 name-derived IDs in the file; grouping by ID may split at most one person across folds), single site, English speakers, sustained vowel only, no external validation.
- Published accuracies on this dataset are inflated by recording-level splits; we split by subject.
- Phone microphones, rooms and codecs differ from the original recordings.
- PD medication state, age and sex are not modelled; the healthy group is small (8).
