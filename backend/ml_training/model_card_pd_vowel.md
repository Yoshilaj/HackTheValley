# {{model_version}} — Parkinson's sustained-vowel classifier (EXPERIMENTAL)

> **Research prototype. Not a diagnostic device.** The output is a task-specific
> score used only as one input to a reviewed follow-up rule (PD-03); it never
> raises a finding on its own and must never be presented as a diagnosis.

Generated by `ml_training/train_pd_vowel.py` on {{created_utc}}.

## Intended use
- Research / hackathon prototype in a health-advocate app: estimate whether a
  sustained /a/ phonation resembles recordings from people with Parkinson's in
  the UCI dataset, as a *supporting* signal for suggesting a clinical follow-up.
- Out of scope: diagnosis, screening of the general population, severity
  estimation, any clinical decision, non-English speakers, connected speech.

## Training data
- UCI Parkinson's dataset (Little et al., IEEE TBME 2009; BioMed Eng OnLine 2007):
  **{{n_recordings}} recordings; documented as 31 subjects (23 with PD)**, ~6 sustained-vowel
  phonations per subject. **Subject-ID caveat:** the dataset documentation says
  31 people (23 PD), but deriving the subject from the `name` column
  (`phon_R01_S01_1` → `phon_R01_S01`) yields {{n_subjects}} name-derived IDs ({{n_pd_subjects}} PD-labelled, {{n_healthy_subjects}} healthy);
  three IDs (S21, S27, S35) have 7 recordings instead of 6. We group CV by the {{n_subjects}} IDs,
  so if one person carries two IDs, at most one person may have recordings on both
  sides of a fold. This is *less* strict than true per-person grouping, and the
  reported grouped-CV AUC may therefore be very slightly optimistic.
- Sustained vowel only; English speakers (recorded in Denver, CO, USA); studio
  conditions; small, single-site sample with no external validation.
- Features: the 22 published dysphonia measures — {{features}}.

## Model
- Candidates: StandardScaler + SelectKBest + LogisticRegression, and XGBoost (depth ≤ 3, ≤ 300 trees).
- Chosen: **{{chosen_model}}** (higher pooled grouped-CV AUC); final hyper-parameters `{{best_params}}`.
- Probabilities calibrated with sigmoid `CalibratedClassifierCV` on subject-grouped splits.

## Serving caveat — app scores are experimental / display-only
> **The metrics above were measured on the UCI feature table, not on audio.**
> In the app, features are computed from phone recordings by
> `app/ml/features/vowel.py` using Praat (parselmouth) plus our own
> re-implementations of RPDE, DFA, PPE, spread1/spread2 and D2. `spread2`, `D2`
> and `NHR` are known to be on a **different scale** from the UCI/MDVP training
> features, and **no validation on real audio exists** (no recording has been run
> through both pipelines). App probabilities are therefore experimental,
> display-only values: they are never a diagnosis, never raise a finding alone
> (rule PD-03 requires an independent supporting rule), and their calibration and
> threshold should not be assumed to transfer from the table above.

## Serving-time feature differences (stated limitation)

## Limitations
- 31 people ({{n_subjects}} name-derived IDs in the file; grouping by ID may split at most one person across folds), single site, English speakers, sustained vowel only, no external validation.
- Published accuracies on this dataset are inflated by recording-level splits; we split by subject.
- Phone microphones, rooms and codecs differ from the original recordings.
- PD medication state, age and sex are not modelled; the healthy group is small (8).
