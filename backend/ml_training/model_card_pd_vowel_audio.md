# {{model_version}} — Parkinson's sustained-vowel classifier trained on real audio (EXPERIMENTAL)

> **Research prototype. Not a diagnostic device.** The output is a task-specific
> score used only as one input to a reviewed follow-up rule (PD-03); it never
> raises a finding on its own and must never be presented as a diagnosis.

Generated by `ml_training/train_pd_vowel_audio.py` on {{created_utc}}.

## Why this model exists
`pd_vowel_experimental_v1` was trained on UCI's pre-computed MDVP feature table while
the app computes its own features from audio. This model is trained on **features
computed by the app's own extractor** (`app/ml/features/vowel.py::extract_vowel_features`,
after the app's `assess_quality` gate) from real sustained-/a/ recordings, so **there is
no train/serve feature skew**: the number the app computes at serving time is the same
kind of number the model saw in training.

## Intended use
- Research / hackathon prototype in a health-advocate app: estimate whether a sustained
  /a/ phonation resembles recordings from people with Parkinson's in the training set,
  as a *supporting* signal for suggesting a clinical follow-up.
- Out of scope: diagnosis, screening of the general population, severity estimation,
  any clinical decision, connected speech.

## Limitations
- Small single-site sample ({{n_subjects}} people), Italian speakers, sustained vowel only,
  no external validation; the CIs are wide and the threshold is unstable.
- **Channel confound**: healthy controls and PD patients were recorded on different
  channels / sessions (see the session table). Shimmer, NHR, HNR, RPDE, DFA and D2 differ
  by channel, so part of the discrimination may be channel, not disease. The
  "without F0" and single-sample-rate rows do not remove this confound.
- Phone microphones, rooms and codecs in the app differ from both recording channels here.
- PD patients were on medication (ON state); age and sex are not modelled.
- The quality gate excluded recordings unevenly across labels ({{gate_failures_by_label}}).
